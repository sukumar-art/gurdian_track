# Constitution – CollabEscrow (Startup Influencer Marketplace)

## 1. Purpose
CollabEscrow exists to bridge startups and influencers through a transparent, trust‑driven marketplace. Our mission is to simplify collaboration, ensure fair pricing, and guarantee secure payments via escrow.

## 2. Core Principles
- **Transparency**: All influencer rate cards are visible upfront, eliminating hidden costs.
- **Trust**: Escrow ensures startups pay only after deliverables are approved.
- **Fairness**: Influencers receive timely payouts with clear commission structures.
- **Accessibility**: Startups of all sizes can discover and engage influencers without gatekeeping.
- **Scalability**: Designed to grow from local pilots to national and international reach.

## 3. Governance
- **Founding Entity**: Operated under Apticraze Intelligence LLP.
- **Decision Authority**: Strategic direction led by Founder; operational execution delegated to Antigravity development team.
- **Documentation Hierarchy**:
  1. Constitution (this document)
  2. Product Bible
  3. UI/UX Document
  4. Technical Standards
  5. PRD (Product Requirements Document)

## 4. Stakeholders
- **Startups**: Gain access to verified influencers with transparent pricing.
- **Influencers**: Monetize reach with secure payments and KYC‑verified trust.
- **Platform**: Earn commission + escrow fee while maintaining neutrality.
- **Admin Team**: Oversees KYC, transaction monitoring, and dispute resolution.

## 5. Operating Model
- **Escrow Payments**: Razorpay integration holds funds until approval.
- **Commission Policy**: 10–15% commission + ₹99 escrow fee per transaction.
- **Verification**: Influencer KYC mandatory before marketplace listing.
- **Data Integrity**: Supabase database ensures secure storage of user and transaction data.

## 6. Brand Identity
- **Name**: CollabEscrow
- **Tone**: Professional, premium, startup‑friendly.
- **Design Language**: Dark mode, slate‑indigo base, neon accents for trust and modernity.
- **Promise**: “Collaboration made transparent, payments made secure.”

## 7. Amendments
This Constitution may be updated as CollabEscrow evolves. Amendments require Founder approval and must be documented in version‑controlled repositories.
