# Product Bible – CollabEscrow (Startup Influencer Marketplace)

## 1. Vision
CollabEscrow is designed to be the trusted bridge between startups and influencers. Our vision is to democratize influencer marketing by making it transparent, affordable, and secure for emerging brands, while ensuring influencers are paid fairly and on time.

## 2. Mission
- Empower startups to discover and collaborate with influencers without hidden costs.
- Provide influencers with a reliable platform to monetize their reach.
- Build trust through escrow payments, transparent rate cards, and verified KYC.

## 3. Product Philosophy
- **Transparency First**: No hidden fees, no opaque negotiations. Rate cards are visible upfront.
- **Trust Layer**: Escrow ensures startups only release funds after deliverables are approved.
- **Lean & Scalable**: Built on free‑tier tools (Vercel, Supabase, Razorpay) to minimize costs while enabling rapid scaling.
- **Startup‑Friendly**: Designed for small budgets, simple onboarding, and fast execution.
- **Influencer‑Centric**: Fair payouts, verified profiles, and clear commission policies.

## 4. Core Features (Phase 1 MVP)
1. **Startup Onboarding**: Signup, budget entry, goal definition, dashboard access.
2. **Influencer Onboarding**: Profile creation, rate card upload, KYC verification.
3. **Marketplace**: Search/filter influencers by niche, region, budget, follower tier.
4. **Escrow Payments**: Razorpay integration, secure hold, commission + fee deduction.
5. **Admin Panel**: KYC approvals, transaction monitoring, CSV export.
6. **Seed Data**: 50 influencers (food + education niches), 20 startups (ZORIA + UNIVISTA pilots).

## 5. Roadmap
### Phase 1 (MVP – July 2026)
- Core onboarding flows.
- Marketplace search + booking.
- Escrow payment integration.
- Admin panel with KYC + CSV export.
- Pilot launch with seeded data.

### Phase 2 (Expansion – Q4 2026)
- Advanced filters (engagement rate, audience demographics).
- Automated influencer verification via APIs.
- Dispute resolution workflows.
- Analytics dashboard for startups (ROI tracking).
- Influencer performance metrics.

### Phase 3 (Scale – 2027)
- Mobile app (React Native).
- Multi‑currency support for international expansion.
- AI‑driven influencer recommendations.
- Subscription tiers for startups (premium features).
- API integrations with startup incubators and marketing platforms.

## 6. Business Model
- **Revenue Streams**:
  - Commission: 10–15% per transaction.
  - Escrow Fee: Flat ₹99 per transaction.
  - Future: Premium subscription tiers for startups.
- **Cost Structure**:
  - Lean infra (Vercel, Supabase free tiers).
  - Razorpay transaction fees.
  - Minimal admin overhead.

## 7. Brand Promise
CollabEscrow promises startups: *“Collaboration made transparent, payments made secure.”*  
For influencers: *“Your work, your worth — paid on time, every time.”*
