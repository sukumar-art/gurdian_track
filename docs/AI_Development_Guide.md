# AI Development Guide – CollabEscrow (Startup Influencer Marketplace)

## 1. Purpose
This guide defines how artificial intelligence (AI) modules will be integrated into CollabEscrow. The goal is to enhance discovery, trust, and efficiency without compromising transparency or security.

---

## 2. Guiding Principles
- **Assistive, Not Dominant**: AI supports decision-making but does not replace human judgment.
- **Transparency**: AI recommendations must be explainable and auditable.
- **Privacy**: No sensitive user data is exposed or shared outside the platform.
- **Scalability**: AI modules should be modular, allowing incremental upgrades.
